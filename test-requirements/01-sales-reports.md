# Test requirement 1: Sales reports

**Paste this into DesignPilot:**

Our field sales team can't find their monthly reports quickly in the sales portal. They visit clients all day and mostly use their phones. Reports are hidden three levels deep under "Analytics > Exports > Monthly". Many support tickets complain about this. Can we fix this?

**What a good result looks like**
- Phase 1 mentions phones, managers, offline use, and permissions.
- Phase 2 has sources for every claim and a short interview guide.
- Phase 3 offers really different directions (for example a home shortcut, a reports hub, and automatic monthly emails).
- Phase 5 gives a prototype where you can open last month's report in 2 taps.
